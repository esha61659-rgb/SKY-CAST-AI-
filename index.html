About eSkyCast AI

eSkyCast AI is a responsive weather dashboard built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It retrieves forecast information from Open-Meteo and turns it into an easy-to-read interface.

  • City search and quick city selection
  • Current weather information
  • Five-day weather forecast
  • Mobile-friendly navigation
  • Weather tips based on forecast data
